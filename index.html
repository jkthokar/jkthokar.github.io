About

I'm a student in the Department of Artificial Intelligence at Kyungdong University Global (KDU), where my work sits mostly in applied deep learning and computer vision — usually as part of a supervised group project, occasionally as something I take further on my own. I like problems where a model has to deal with messy, real input: a face under bad lighting, a hand moving mid-air, a sentence full of slang. Outside of coursework, I also build and ship small production websites, most recently a full inventory and stock-tracking system for a company, wired up end to end with authentication and a live database.
